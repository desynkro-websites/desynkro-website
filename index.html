ABOUT ME GET TO KNOW ME

Hey, I'm DeSynkro! Jack of all trades and Linux gamer running CachyOS (Arch btw).

I build websites, shoot and edit photos, edit videos, and occasionally pretend I know what I'm doing in cybersecurity. I'm deep in the Linux rabbit hole, self-hosting, tinkering, and pushing what open-source can do.

I used to stream Just Chatting on Twitch and made tutorials on YouTube teaching others the same. These days I help people switch to Linux and get as much of their games and software running as possible.

In my free time, you'll find me gaming, achievement hunting, or learning different Linux distros.

If you'd like to work together on a project, collaborate, or hire me for any of my services, don't hesitate to reach out!

Hey there, I'm Iago Rosa!

I was born and raised in Brazil and moved to Perth, Australia with my family in 2006 and have proudly called this country my home ever since.

I've always been a massive tech and gaming nerd. My all-time favourite game is The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim (AKA "Mod it until it crashes"). I love modding games and achievement hunting. I used to play a lot of First Person Shooters and Counter Strike competitively, but these days I'm more into fun single-player and co-op games I can enjoy with friends. Feel free to add me on STEAM if you want to game together!

In 2020 I gave streaming and content creation a try and managed to turn it into a career. After a year of consistent grinding I became a Twitch Partner. During that time I met the love of my life, and after Covid I flew out to the Philippines to be with her. We're happily married now.

I'm also really into photography, especially portraits, I love taking photos of people, and automotive shots. My favorite cars are the Nissan Skyline R34 and the Mazda RX-7.

At home I've got my little puppy Luna (Maltese Shih Tzu) No matter how old she gets, she'll always be a puppy to me, you'll definitely spot her in the portfolio!

I'm a strong privacy advocate, I believe mandatory ID verification online creates more problems than it solves, especially with how common data breaches and leaks are. That passion led me down the rabbit hole of cybersecurity, self-hosting, open source software, and Linux.

In late 2023, Microslop's awful business practices finally pushed me over the edge. I started with Linux Mint, fell in love with the whole ecosystem, and by 2025 I had fully migrated to CachyOS (arch btw). All my systems are now running Linux.

These days I work as a tradie. With the rise of AI making me fear about the long-term future of my tech and photography work, I wanted something more "AI-resistant", plus the constant movement keeps me fit, which I love. I'm big into gym sessions and nutrition too.

I'm just a chill guy who enjoys life, good games, and learning new things. Thanks for stopping by, I hope you enjoy your stay!
